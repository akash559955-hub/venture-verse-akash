Founded with a vision to impart world-class technical and professional education, Oriental Group of Institutes (OGI), Bhopal stands as a premier educational conglomerate known for academic excellence, innovation, and industry leadership in Central India.

Our Annual Techno-Management Event

ABOUT VENTURE VERSE

Venture Verse is the premier annual techno-management event organized by the Department of CSBS & CSE at OGI Bhopal. Conceived as a battleground for innovators, it celebrates the powerful convergence of technical development, financial acumen, and executive presence.

Spanning four flagship arenas — Venture Craft (Ideathon), Mudra (7-Day Virtual Currency Trading), Karvaan (Corporate Ramp Walk), and UNIFEST (The Startup Carnival) — the event brings together visionaries, algorithmic thinkers, and leaders ready to create real-world impact.
